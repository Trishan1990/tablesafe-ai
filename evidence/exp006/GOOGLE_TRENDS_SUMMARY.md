# EXP-006 Google Trends Summary

## Observation Window

November 1, 2025 – January 31, 2026

## Positive Signals

### moringa salmonella

Search interest increased significantly during November 2025, prior to the January 2026 recall.

### moringa side effects

Search activity remained elevated throughout the observation period and increased approaching January 2026.

### salmonella supplement

Search interest showed a significant spike during November 2025, prior to the recall period.

## Negative Signals

### moringa food poisoning

No measurable Google Trends activity detected during the observation period.

### super greens supplement sick

No measurable Google Trends activity detected during the observation period.

## Initial Interpretation

Three of five monitored search terms demonstrated measurable public interest before or near the recall period. Two terms showed no measurable activity. These results suggest that keyword selection materially affects early-warning performance and that consumer search behavior may provide useful weak-signal indicators for contamination events.
